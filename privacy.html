Privacy Policy

Little Stone Games (Andrii Kaminskyi) · Effective 25 October 2026

This policy explains what information our mobile and web games collect, why, and what choices you have. It applies to all games published by Little Stone Games, the developer name of Andrii Kaminskyi, including Goods Sort, Shelf Sort Master, Parcel Sort, Pixel Painters and Toy Box Exit, and to future games.

What we collect ourselves

Our games have no accounts and do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, or location. Your game progress (level, coins, stars, sound setting) is stored only on your device. If you delete the game or clear its data, that progress is lost.

Ads and your choices

  • In the EEA, UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change that choice by reinstalling the game or clearing its data.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Rewarded ads are always optional. You choose when to watch one in exchange for an in-game bonus.

Children

Our games are made for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal data about you, and to object to its processing. Because we do not hold accounts or personal data ourselves, most requests are handled by the services listed above, but we will help you with any request.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date at the top.
